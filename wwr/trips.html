Our Trips

Choose the adventure that fits your group. Every trip includes a safety briefing, a certified guide, and all the gear you need.

Featured Trips

Calm Waters Float

A relaxed paddle through gentle Class I–II rapids, with time to spot wildlife and enjoy the scenery. Our guides teach basic paddling skills at the start, so no experience is needed. This trip is ideal for families with young children and first-time rafters.

Canyon Run

Wind through a narrow canyon with fast Class III sections, splashy waves, and a lunch stop on the riverbank. Teamwork matters here, so your guide coaches the group on timing and commands. A great fit for active groups and teens.

Big Wave Rapids

Our most thrilling full-day trip, packed with big Class IV waves and technical rapids. Paddlers should be comfortable in moving water and in good physical shape. It ends with a team celebration on the shore.

Sunset Float

An evening trip as the light turns golden over the river. The water is calm and the pace is easy, making it a favorite for couples and small groups looking for something memorable and scenic.
